# Phase 5 UI/UX Guide

AimAze Laundry ERP now uses a premium SaaS-style interface for manager, counter, driver, and customer-facing workflows.

## Modernized Screens

- Executive Dashboard: KPI cards, alert strips, and premium command-center layout.
- Quick Counter Order: touch-friendly counter shell with clearer service and payment flow.
- Laundry Orders: smart buttons, workflow timeline, modern kanban cards, urgency badges.
- Garment Tracking: lifecycle timeline, barcode badges, QC and rewash indicators.
- Driver Jobs: delivery-app style cards with status, address, cash collection, and proof workflow.
- Customer Portal: cards for wallet, subscriptions, orders, pickup, complaints, and garment tracking.
- Reports: cleaner headers, branch/TRN display, modern tables, and receipt-style summaries.

Keep high-volume screens fast by relying on CSS and server-side views instead of heavy browser rendering.
