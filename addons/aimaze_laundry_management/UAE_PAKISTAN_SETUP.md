# UAE and Pakistan Setup Guide

## UAE

1. Set company country to United Arab Emirates.
2. Set currency to AED.
3. Configure VAT 5% sales tax.
4. Assign VAT to applicable laundry services/products.
5. Configure AED journals for cash, bank, and sales.

## Pakistan

1. Set company country to Pakistan.
2. Set currency to PKR.
3. Configure sales tax manually according to client registration/province.
4. Assign configured Pakistan tax to services/products.
5. Configure PKR journals for cash, bank, and sales.

No country-specific account codes are hardcoded by the module.
