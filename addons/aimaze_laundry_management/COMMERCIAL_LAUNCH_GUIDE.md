# Commercial Launch Guide

## Positioning

AimAze Laundry ERP is a GCC-ready laundry operations, accounting, delivery, and customer engagement platform powered by Odoo.

## Target Industries

- Retail laundries
- Dry cleaners
- Hotel linen operations
- Salons, gyms, restaurants, and corporate uniform services
- Multi-branch laundry chains

## SaaS Pricing Suggestions

- Starter: single branch, basic orders, portal, reports.
- Professional: multi-branch, wallet, subscriptions, driver workflows.
- Enterprise: corporate billing, advanced accounting, APIs, WhatsApp connector, custom reports.

## Implementation Process

1. Discovery and branch/accounting setup.
2. Services, pricing, taxes, and receipt configuration.
3. Staff roles and training.
4. Pilot with sample orders.
5. Go-live and daily support.
6. Monthly optimization review.

## Support Model

Offer onboarding, remote support, backup monitoring, minor configuration changes, and upgrade windows.
